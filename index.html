THE PROBLEM

A 3D asset is not a file.It is a living composition.

A 3D asset keeps evolving throughout its lifetime. Its authored dimensions and downstream representations do not stand still.

Most workflows still manage that change through disconnected files, copies, and exports. The result is fragmentation, asset drift, and lost trust.

Traditional workflows optimize for delivery. Assets do not stop evolving.
THE MODEL

One asset.One evolving authoritative state.

XYZO creates a canonical asset whose dimensions can evolve independently while remaining part of the same trusted whole.
