Category

Specialized tools keep their roles. XYZO preserves the asset across them.

Creation tools author. Pipelines move work. Publishing systems deliver. XYZO maintains identity, continuity, and trusted state across them.

Creation tools

They own geometry, materials, textures, animation, and design intent. XYZO preserves how those outputs continue to belong to the same asset.

Pipeline and production systems

They own tasks, approvals, schedules, versions, and handoffs. XYZO maintains the trusted state of the asset being moved.

Conversion and optimization tools

They transform content for a purpose. XYZO keeps those operations connected to canonical state.

Publishing systems

They deliver derived representations from canonical state. They do not create another competing authority.
