THE PROBLEM

A 3D asset is not a file. It is a living composition.

Geometry, materials, textures, variants, metadata, dependencies, and downstream representations continue to evolve throughout an asset’s lifetime. Most workflows still manage them as disconnected files, copies, and exports, creating fragmentation, asset drift, and lost trust.

Traditional workflows optimize for delivery. Assets do not stop evolving.That mismatch compounds as assets move across tools, contributors, and downstream uses.
THE MODEL

One asset. One evolving authoritative state.

XYZO creates a canonical asset whose geometry, materials, textures, metadata, variants, and dependencies can evolve independently while remaining part of the same trusted whole.
