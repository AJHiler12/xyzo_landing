# XYZO Website Narrative

## Purpose

This document is the narrative authority for the public XYZO homepage.

It governs:

- homepage section order
- homepage messaging hierarchy
- public versus protected content boundaries
- CTA hierarchy
- progressive introduction of technical concepts

It does not replace product truth.

`PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md` and `PRODUCT_POSITIONING.md` remain the higher authorities for what XYZO is, what it is not, and which claims are accurate.

This document defines how that truth should be expressed on the public landing page.

---

## What The Public Homepage Is For

The public homepage is the 30,000-foot introduction to XYZO.

Its job is to explain, clearly and succinctly:

1. what XYZO is
2. what problem it solves
3. how the canonical asset model works at a high level
4. how the product acts on the asset
5. what is already working
6. what category responsibility XYZO owns
7. why the problem matters now
8. how to request deeper access

The homepage should summarize and orient.

It should not reproduce the First Principles deck or the Advisor deck word for word.

---

## Intended Audience

The public homepage is for thoughtful visitors who need an accurate introduction before deeper discussion.

That includes:

- advisors
- domain experts
- technical collaborators
- early users
- partners
- informed investors

The homepage should be understandable to an intelligent non-specialist without becoming simplistic.

It should not assume the visitor already understands OpenUSD, canonical asset models, or XYZO's internal architecture vocabulary.

---

## Narrative Rules

The homepage must:

- lead with product responsibility before technical detail
- introduce concepts in digestible layers
- preserve the distinction between working capabilities and future scope
- keep product claims aligned with approved truth
- use real product evidence wherever possible
- make the public/protected split explicit

The homepage must not:

- behave like a direct deck transcription
- front-load deep authority-model language before the visitor understands the problem
- imply that XYZO is primarily a converter, viewer, pipeline tracker, or publishing utility
- imply that future capabilities are complete
- require the visitor to understand detailed architecture before grasping the core idea

---

## Homepage Structure

The homepage narrative should follow this eight-section structure.

### 1. Hero

Purpose:

- answer what XYZO is
- answer what XYZO does
- introduce the core model

Approved hierarchy:

- Eyebrow: `INFRASTRUCTURE FOR LIVING 3D ASSETS`
- Headline: `Keep 3D assets trustworthy as they move.`
- Supporting line: `One canonical asset. Many evolving dimensions. Many trusted representations.`

Interpretation:

- the eyebrow explains what XYZO is
- the headline explains what XYZO does
- the supporting line introduces the architectural model in compressed form

Hero guidance:

- keep the copy concise
- do not replace the headline with a more technical explanation
- do not introduce OpenUSD in the hero
- use the CTA to invite deeper access rather than force a sale

### 2. The Problem

Primary idea:

`A 3D asset is not a file. It is a living composition.`

Purpose:

- reframe the visitor's mental model
- explain why file-centered workflows break down

Suggested direction:

- geometry, materials, textures, variants, metadata, dependencies, and outputs continue to evolve
- most workflows still manage them as disconnected files, copies, and exports
- that creates fragmentation, asset drift, and lost trust

Rules:

- define the problem concretely
- avoid abstract manifesto language
- do not assume the visitor already agrees with the phrase "living composition"

### 3. The Model

Primary idea:

`One asset. One evolving authoritative state.`

Purpose:

- explain how XYZO thinks about the asset
- introduce the model without overwhelming the visitor

The public homepage should introduce only these three concepts at this stage:

- Persistent identity
- Evolving dimensions
- Trusted representations

Suggested direction:

- XYZO creates a canonical asset
- geometry, materials, textures, metadata, variants, and dependencies can evolve independently
- they remain part of the same trusted whole

Rules:

- do not introduce the full authority model too early
- do not lead with source evidence, runtime intent, and derived representations unless simplified through a clear diagram or tightly controlled copy
- keep the explanation visual and digestible

### 4. The Product

Primary idea:

`XYZO coordinates the asset as it moves and evolves.`

Purpose:

- explain what the product does
- connect the abstract model to product behavior

Required operating loop:

- Normalize
- Compose
- Inspect
- Synchronize
- Publish

Rules:

- treat this as an ordered operating loop
- explain each step in plain language
- do not turn the loop into generic feature marketing
- preserve the distinction between current capabilities and future expansion

### 5. Evidence

Primary idea:

`The architecture is already working.`

Purpose:

- prove the product is real
- ground the narrative in evidence

Evidence should prioritize:

- Forge screenshots
- synchronization before/after proof
- canonical package and inspection proof
- concise capability-status labels

Approved status framing includes:

- `Working today`
- `In active development`

Rules:

- use product proof, not abstract promise
- do not pad this section with speculative roadmap language
- do not present future publishing profiles, broader automation, APIs, or team workflows as complete

### 6. Category Responsibility

Primary idea:

`Specialized tools should remain specialized.`

Purpose:

- explain where XYZO sits
- prevent category confusion

Required distinctions:

- creation tools own authoring
- pipelines own workflow
- DAM/PIM systems own records and distribution
- publishing tools own delivery
- XYZO owns the continuity and trusted state of the 3D asset itself

Key statement:

`XYZO takes responsibility for the continuity of the 3D asset itself.`

Rules:

- keep the boundary explanation clear
- do not let XYZO collapse into adjacent categories

### 7. Why Now

Primary idea:

`The coordination problem is becoming unavoidable.`

Purpose:

- explain why this matters now rather than eventually

Suggested direction:

- creation is fragmenting
- consumption is multiplying
- asset change is accelerating
- representations are growing faster than teams can maintain trust between them

Rules:

- keep the urgency grounded
- avoid hype and unsupported market claims

### 8. Closing And Access

Primary idea:

`The future of 3D should not be constrained by the limitations of files.`

Supporting line:

`The infrastructure for that future does not exist yet. We are building it.`

Purpose:

- end on the larger conviction
- point the visitor toward the right next step

CTA hierarchy:

- Primary CTA: `Request Early Access`
- Secondary path: `Explore the thinking behind XYZO`

Rules:

- primary CTA should invite contact or access request
- secondary CTA should orient the visitor toward deeper material rather than duplicate the homepage

## Public Versus Protected Content

The public website should summarize and orient.

The deeper materials should eventually live in protected areas:

- `/principles/`
- `/advisor/`

The homepage should make those deeper bodies of thinking legible without reproducing them in full.

---

## `/principles/`

The public preview for `/principles/` should explain that this area contains the foundational beliefs behind XYZO.

That includes:

- why a 3D asset should behave as a living composition
- why identity must persist through change
- why connected infrastructure matters

Protected content should preserve the First Principles deck word for word.

---

## `/advisor/`

The public preview for `/advisor/` should explain that this area contains:

- product architecture
- canonical asset model
- current MVP capabilities
- category boundaries
- roadmap and expansion
- founder context
- strategic questions

Protected content should preserve the Advisor deck word for word.

---

## Progressive Technical Disclosure

The homepage should move from legible public framing to deeper technical language gradually.

Preferred sequence:

1. explain the problem
2. explain the asset model simply
3. explain what XYZO does
4. show evidence
5. clarify category boundaries
6. offer deeper access

Do not force the visitor to parse these concepts before the problem is clear:

- source evidence
- runtime intent
- full authority-model distinctions
- publishing projection details
- detailed OpenUSD mechanics

Those concepts remain valid, but they belong later in the narrative or inside protected deeper material.

---

## Proof And Capability Rules

The homepage should use current, supportable capability language.

It must clearly distinguish:

- working today
- in active development
- future direction

It must not imply that all target-specific publishing profiles, APIs, team workflows, geometry synchronization, broader synchronization, or broader automation are already complete.

When capability detail is shown:

- use concise labels
- attach proof wherever possible
- preserve the difference between architecture already proven and expansion still underway

---

## CTA Rules

CTA behavior should match product maturity.

Preferred CTA pattern:

- public homepage explains the product and invites a deeper conversation
- deeper material is requested or protected rather than dumped into the public page

Do not use:

- aggressive sales language
- fake urgency
- mature-platform navigation that implies broad availability
- duplicate CTAs that do not lead anywhere meaningful

---

## Phrases And Patterns To Avoid

Avoid:

- direct deck section names presented as final public narrative by default
- homepage copy that reads like architecture notes
- early overuse of `source evidence`, `canonical state`, `runtime intent`, and `derived representations` without setup
- language that makes XYZO sound primarily like a pipeline tool
- language that makes XYZO sound primarily like a converter
- unexplained repetition of `composition` before the visitor understands what is being composed
- route assumptions that collapse public and protected material into one surface

Avoid generic labels when a sentence-based idea is stronger.

Prefer:

- declarative section ideas
- clear responsibility statements
- short explanatory body copy beneath them

---

## Acceptable Repetition

Some repetition is useful because the homepage is introducing a new category.

It is acceptable to restate these ideas across the page:

- a 3D asset is not a file
- identity persists while state evolves
- specialized tools should remain specialized
- XYZO owns the continuity of the 3D asset itself
- the asset can have one authoritative state and many trusted representations

Repetition should reinforce understanding, not add new terminology each time.

---

## Relationship To Higher Authorities

This document translates higher-authority product truth into homepage narrative.

Use the hierarchy this way:

- `PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md` defines non-negotiable product truth
- `PRODUCT_POSITIONING.md` defines what XYZO is and is not
- `WEBSITE_NARRATIVE.md` defines how the public homepage expresses that truth
- `WEBSITE_GOALS.md` defines what the website must accomplish operationally

If homepage copy conflicts with product truth, product truth wins.

If multiple homepage options fit product truth, this document governs the preferred narrative and flow.
