Plate I — The problem

A 3D asset is not a file.

It is a living composition.

Assets move across creation tools, conversion systems, production pipelines, review environments, publishing targets, and downstream uses. They are copied, converted, optimized, renamed, repackaged, and modified.

As they move, dimensions evolve without coordination, dependencies drift, outputs become disconnected, and origin becomes difficult to trace.

Plate II — The canonical asset

The canonical asset is the product foundation.

Source evidence records where the asset began. Canonical state defines what the asset is now. Derived representations express how the asset is used.
